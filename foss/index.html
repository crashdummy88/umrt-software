FOSS

Open-source candidates

These may be mirrored under /mirrors/ only after license check, pinned version, and SHA256. Proprietary vendor firmware never lands here.

  1. Confirm license allows redistribution.
  2. Pin an upstream release tag (not a random commit).
  3. Record SHA256 + upstream URL in catalog/index.json with policy: "mirror".
  4. Store the artifact under mirrors/<id>/<version>/.
