SureCall · existing boosters

Official SureCall support — same RF rules as weBoost

SureCall vehicle and building boosters appear on coaches we did not originally spec. United Mobile RV is a weBoost Authorized Installer for new cellular work. We will still diagnose a SureCall that is already installed: donor antenna, isolation, and whether SureCall’s published app or firmware note applies. We do not claim a SureCall partner title, and we do not re-host their packages.

Manufacturer start: surecall.com/support

When SureCall’s software helps — and when it does not

Use SureCall’s official support or app when the amplifier has power and you need a published LED chart, app pairing step, or firmware note for that exact model. Software will not create outdoor RF. If the donor sees nothing, or indoor and outdoor antennas are oscillating, the fix is placement, cable integrity, and isolation — the same physics as weBoost.

Existing SureCall

Confirm fused DC, antenna isolation, and the model sticker. Then follow SureCall’s current article. Do not flash a “booster unlock” file.

New cellular install

We quote weBoost RV kits with planned roof real estate. That is the credential we hold and the install we document.
