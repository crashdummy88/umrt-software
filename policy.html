Mirror vs link-out

  • Open source — Mirror only when the license allows redistribution. Keep upstream URL, version, and SHA256. See mirrors/README.md.
  • Vendor firmware — Link to official manufacturer portals only. No closed .bin / .swu / vendor zips in this repo or on Pages.
  • Apps — Official App Store, Play, or vendor desktop installers only.
  • No cracks, keygens, or unlocked firmware.
  • Credentials — Exact titles in text only; no partner logo images.
