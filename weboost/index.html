Field honesty

  • Outdoor RF required — a booster needs a usable donor signal outside. Inside a metal coach with no outdoor path, expect little or nothing.
  • AGC — weBoost units use automatic gain control. Field work is placement, isolation, and cable integrity — not inventing a manual gain dial that isn’t there.
  • No invent-from-zero — if outdoor RF is absent or drowned in noise, software and apps cannot create bars.
  • No “tune gain” — we do not claim custom gain tuning as a product. Follow official install guidance and LED / app feedback.
  • Authorized Installer — exact credential wording. No invented weBoost partner tiers on this hub.
