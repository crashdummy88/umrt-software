RF honesty (Authorized Installer)

  • Outdoor / donor RF first — place and aim the outdoor antenna where the phone already sees a weak usable signal.
  • Isolation — indoor and outdoor antennas must not hear each other; oscillation trips protection / AGC and can be worse than no booster.
  • AGC — automatic gain control; field work is placement, coax, fused DC, and isolation — not a gain knob.
  • Multi-carrier — weBoost-class kits boost what is present across supported bands; they do not create coverage from dead zones.
  • App — aiming / before-after readings only; it does not invent outdoor RF.
