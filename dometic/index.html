Dometic · appliances & climate

Official Dometic apps and support

United Mobile RV is a Dometic Professional Certified field shop. Climate, refrigeration, and related Dometic systems show up on the same coaches we already diagnose for power and connectivity. This page points at Dometic’s own apps and support — not a firmware mirror, not a third-party “unlock.”

Manufacturer start: dometic.com

When the official app is the right next step

Use Dometic’s published app or support portal when the control board, thermostat, or mobile pairing is the question — firmware notes, fault-code lookup, and store installers. Use the company Field Guide when the symptom is electrical (shore lost, converter dead, lithium not accepting charge) or when the appliance fault is secondary to a power problem. An A/C that will not start after a lithium conversion is often a soft-start, surge, or inverter-limit issue, not a missing Dometic binary.

Apps & pairing

Install Dometic’s current store listing for the product family on the coach. We do not invent App Store IDs or sideload APKs. If pairing fails after a board swap, confirm the exact model in Dometic support before chasing a “newer” unofficial package.

Fault codes

The Field Guide keeps an OEM fault-code index that includes Dometic alongside Onan. Read the code on the unit, then match it to Dometic’s document for that series — not a generic blog table.

When to stop and call

Gas appliances, sealed refrigeration, and rooftop climate involve leak, fire, and crush hazards. We come to the campsite or storage lot. Text the model sticker, the code, and whether shore or inverter is up.
