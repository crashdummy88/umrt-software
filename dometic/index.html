Dometic · appliances & climate

Official Dometic apps and support

United Mobile RV is a Dometic Professional Certified field shop. Climate, refrigeration, and related Dometic systems show up on the same coaches we already diagnose for power and connectivity. This page points at Dometic’s own apps and support — not a firmware mirror, not a third-party “unlock.”

Manufacturer start: dometic.com

When the official app is the right next step

Use Dometic’s published app or support portal when the control board, thermostat, or mobile pairing is the question — firmware notes, fault-code lookup, and store installers. If the appliance is dark because the inverter, converter, or lithium bank is down, start on Victron software — that is not a missing Dometic binary.

Apps & pairing

Install Dometic’s current store listing for the product family on the coach. We do not invent App Store IDs or sideload APKs. If pairing fails after a board swap, confirm the exact model in Dometic support before chasing a “newer” unofficial package.

Fault codes

Read the code on the unit, then match it to Dometic’s document for that series — not a generic blog table.

When the app is the wrong door

Gas appliances, sealed refrigeration, and rooftop climate are not a firmware flash. Text the model sticker, the code, and whether shore or inverter is up.

Related software

Victron software

If the A/C or fridge is dark because the inverter or lithium bank is down, VictronConnect / GX / VRM is the tool — not a Dometic update.
