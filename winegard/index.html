Winegard · RV antennas & ConnecT

Official Winegard support and apps

Winegard ConnecT hubs and rooftop antennas are already on a lot of coaches we see. United Mobile RV is not a Winegard partner title — we are the shop that diagnoses whether the failure is the app, the hub firmware, the outdoor antenna, or the DC feed. Official Winegard portals only. We do not re-host ConnecT images.

Manufacturer start: winegard.com/support

When to use Winegard’s software

Open the official ConnecT / Winegard app or support article when the hub has power, the SSID appears, and you need a published firmware note or factory reset path. Do not flash a file from a forum thread. If the hub never powers, the outdoor antenna is cracked, or the roof boot is open, software is the wrong door — that is connectivity field work.

ConnecT hub

Treat the hub as a travel router with a Winegard support tree. Confirm the exact model (ConnecT 2.0, 4G2, etc.) before you download anything. Vendor firmware is not OpenWrt from openwrt.org and not Peplink InControl.

TV / satellite antennas

Aiming and receiver pairing follow Winegard’s current manual for that dish. We do not invent a “KING vs Winegard” firmware crosswalk. Existing KING owners: see KING.

When we recommend a different path

Owners who need multi-WAN with a named failover or bonding mode usually move toward Peplink plus Starlink bypass and a proper cellular booster. Winegard remains supported for the hardware that is already on the roof.
