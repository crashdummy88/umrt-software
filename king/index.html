KING Connect · existing owners

Support links for hardware already on the roof

United Mobile RV does not sell new KING Connect kits. Deep KING support URLs often 404; the public site is a thin placeholder. If you already own an Extend or similar KING connectivity hub, we will still diagnose it: power, outdoor antenna, and whether the published app still exists. For a new cellular path we specify weBoost as an Authorized Installer.

Manufacturer start: kingconnect.com

When KING software is still useful

Use whatever KING still publishes for your exact model — app, manual, or firmware note — only after you confirm the sticker. Do not flash a “KING” file of unknown origin. Outdoor RF honesty still applies: a KING booster or hub cannot invent tower signal. If the unit is end-of-life and the app is gone, we treat it as hardware triage, not a software upgrade story.

Existing Extend / hub

Confirm 12 V at the hub, antenna continuity, and whether the SSID still appears. Then follow KING’s remaining official article for that SKU. We will not invent a replacement firmware tree.

When we migrate you

New cellular work goes through weBoost with planned isolation. Satellite work goes through Starlink. Multi-WAN goes through Peplink. Those are the systems we install and stand behind.
