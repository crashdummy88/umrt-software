Plans

Residential is a fixed service address — no roam, no in-motion. Roam is the travel product (pause/standby; in-motion only where Starlink says so). The /rv path redirects to Roam. It is not a separate SKU. Compare on service-plans.

Bypass mode

Official bypass is how Starlink hands WAN to a Peplink or other router. Pair with Peplink when you want named SpeedFusion failover or bonding. We do not re-host Starlink firmware.

Specs & accessories

Mini, Flat High Performance, and High Performance kits do not draw the same watts. Read specifications before you size inverter or DC. Prefer official mounts and cables over clone hardware on a roof you cannot afford to leak.
