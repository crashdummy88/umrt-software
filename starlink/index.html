Field notes (clear-sky first)

A roof-mount is hands-off only when the sky is actually clear overhead. Trees win over a fancy mount. A portable dish takes minutes and can walk out of shade — that is still an official kit, not a cracked firmware trick. Poorly sealed roof penetrations are a water problem, not an app problem.

  • Accessories — prefer official mounts/cables and kit install PDFs over clone hardware.
  • Power — Mini vs Flat HP / HP draw differs a lot. Size DC / inverter from official specifications and measure on site. No invented watt matrices here.
  • Bypass — third-party router (usually Peplink) uses official bypass mode for bonding and/or failover (SpeedFusion). Never re-host proprietary firmware.
  • App / offline — official store apps only. Searching, offline, or obstruction: see the troubleshoot cards, then Text / Book if the mount or power path is the real fault.
  • No customer portal or status destination on this hub. Book the install when you want hands on the roof.
