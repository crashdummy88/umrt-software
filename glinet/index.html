GL.iNet · travel routers

Official GL.iNet firmware — vendor CDN only

GL.iNet travel routers show up on coaches as a compact 4G/5G or WAN box. United Mobile RV will integrate one that is already on the rig — fused DC, antenna leads, and honest WAN roles. We are not a GL.iNet partner. Firmware comes from dl.gl-inet.com for that exact model. Vendor OpenWrt-based images are not the same support path as flashing upstream OpenWrt from openwrt.org.

Manufacturer start: dl.gl-inet.com

When to use GL.iNet’s image vs something else

Stay on vendor firmware

If the router is doing its job (WAN, Wi-Fi, maybe a USB modem) and you need a published fix, use GL.iNet’s CDN and app for that SKU. Confirm model and revision on the label. Wrong image bricks the unit.

Do not call it Peplink

GL.iNet multi-WAN is not Hot Failover, Bonding, or Smoothing. When a coach needs a named SpeedFusion mode and InControl, that is a Peplink conversation — and a Peplink Certified Associate install.

Upstream OpenWrt is a different job

Replacing vendor firmware with upstream OpenWrt is a brick-risk and a warranty path of its own. We do not redistribute proprietary “OpenWrt-derived” vendor binaries on this hub.
