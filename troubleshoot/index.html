Apps / firmware / FOSS

VictronConnect · Venus · VRM

App won't connect, GX offline, or VRM blank — official Victron docs first. Field: Victron Professional Certified Installer.

Peplink / Pepwave

Firmware, InControl, or WAN bonding/failover - official Peplink only. Field: bonding and/or failover (SpeedFusion).

weBoost

Booster LED / app / install checks - outdoor RF required; AGC; no invent-from-zero. Official support only.

Starlink app

App login, obstruction, or offline dish - clear-sky installs; official Starlink support only.

Node-RED / Signal K

Flows, dashboard, or Signal K bridge - official Node-RED + Signal K repos only. We help integrate; we don't ship unvetted community flows as product.

Related software

A VictronConnect failure is often VE.Direct or a GX that never joined VRM. A weBoost “no boost” is usually isolation or no outdoor RF. Peplink flaps are often a health-check target, not a missing firmware mirror. Starlink offline is still a sky-view and PoE problem more often than an app setting.
