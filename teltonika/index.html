When to use the wiki vs a service call

Firmware & RMS

Use the wiki page for your exact RUT/TCR SKU. Remote Management System (RMS) is Teltonika’s cloud — not InControl, not NetCloud, not ALMS. Confirm the license you actually own before you assume remote access.

WAN failover is not SpeedFusion

Teltonika can fail over. That is not Peplink Hot Failover / Bonding / Smoothing. Do not promise bonding from a Teltonika chassis we did not spec.

Power and RF still win

Most “router dead” calls on a coach are cigarette-lighter power, a brownout on Starlink PoE, or a SIM that never registered. We measure on site. See connectivity installs.
